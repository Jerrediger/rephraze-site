Support

flick mail puts all your inboxes into one stack you clear one card at a time. Questions or problems? Email jeremy.redigergroup@gmail.com and you'll hear back within a day or two.

Connecting an inbox

  • Gmail and Outlook / Hotmail / Live: tap Add an email account and sign in with Google or Microsoft.
  • Yahoo, AOL, iCloud and other providers: these use an app password, which you create in your provider's account security settings.
  • Just looking? Tap "Try it with a sample inbox" on the first screen to swipe through example emails without signing in.

Swipes

  • Left: archive, into a folder if you like.
  • Right: reply. Optionally let AI draft it using your own AI account.
  • Up: star and keep it in your inbox.
  • Down: delete. Swipe down at the right edge to mark it as spam instead.

Changed your mind? Tap Undo on the message at the bottom of the screen.

Card colours

Green cards are people who matter most to you, worked out on your phone from how often they email and how quickly and fully you reply. Red cards are likely noise. With flick Pro you can tune this for each inbox: senders who are always high or low, keywords, and how much each signal counts.

flick Pro

The free plan includes two inboxes. flick Pro ($2.99/month) adds unlimited inboxes and priority customization. It's billed through your Apple ID and renews monthly until cancelled. Manage or cancel in Settings → your name → Subscriptions. To restore a purchase on a new phone, open flick → Inboxes → Upgrade → Restore purchases.

Removing your data

Removing an inbox in flick deletes its sign-in and everything flick learned about it from your phone. Deleting the app removes everything. See the privacy policy.

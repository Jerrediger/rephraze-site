Privacy Policy

Effective October 22, 2026

flick mail is an iPhone email app that shows the mail from your accounts one card at a time. The short version: there is no flick account and no flick server. Your email goes straight between your phone and your email provider, and nothing about it is sent to us.

Your email

  • When you add an account, you sign in directly with your provider (Google, Microsoft, or your IMAP provider such as Yahoo, AOL or iCloud). flick connects from your phone to that provider to read, archive, star, delete, mark as spam, and send mail when you ask it to.
  • Sign-in tokens and passwords are stored in your iPhone's Keychain. They are never sent to us.
  • Email content is shown on your phone and cached there only as needed. We do not receive, store, or have access to it.

Importance colors

To color cards by importance, flick looks at who emails you and how often and how quickly you reply. This is worked out on your phone and saved on your phone. It never leaves the device.

Writing replies with AI (optional)

If you connect your own AI provider account (OpenRouter, Anthropic, OpenAI or Google Gemini), flick sends the email you are replying to, and any instruction you give, from your phone directly to that provider using your key when you ask for a suggested reply. That provider handles it under its own terms and privacy policy. We do not receive it. If you never connect an AI provider, nothing is sent to one. Your AI key is stored in your iPhone's Keychain.

Google user data

flick's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Gmail data is used only to show and manage your mail in the app at your request. It is not used for advertising, sold, or transferred to anyone except an AI provider you choose to connect, and only when you ask for a reply to be drafted.

What we collect

Nothing. flick has no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs. If you install a test version through TestFlight, Apple may share crash reports and feedback you choose to send, under Apple's terms.

Deleting your data

Removing an account in flick deletes its saved sign-in and what flick learned about it from your phone. Deleting the app removes everything flick stored. You can also revoke flick's access from your Google or Microsoft account settings at any time.

Children

flick mail is not directed at children under 13.

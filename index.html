Say it sweeter, sharper, or shorter.

Rephraze lives inside iMessage. Type what you want to say, pick a tone and a style, and paste the rewrite straight into your message.

How to use it

  1. In Messages, open any conversation and tap the + button next to the text field.
  2. Choose Rephraze. If you don't see it, tap More and switch it on.
  3. Type your message in the panel, tap a tone (Sweet, Funny, Formal…) and a style (Texty, Casual, Gen Z…), then tap Re-write.
  4. Tap Try again for another version, Edit to go back to your words, or Paste to text to drop it into the message field and send.

Tips

  • Long-press a style to make it your default, so it's already picked every time the panel opens.
  • Bubbles reorder themselves by what you use most.
  • If the panel opens as a small card, tap it — that's Messages' compact view, and the card expands into the full editor.

Something not working?

  • "Rephraze is busy" — the rewrite service was briefly unavailable. Wait a moment and tap Re-write again.
  • Panel is blank or gray — this can happen right after an update. Close Messages fully from the app switcher and open it again.
  • Rephraze isn't in the + menu — tap More at the bottom of that menu and turn Rephraze on.
